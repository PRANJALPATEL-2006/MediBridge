Safety Guidelines

1. Prescription Required: Only request or donate medicines with proper medical advice.

2. Check Expiry: Never share expired medicines or without clear labeling.

3. Meet in Public: Hand over medicines in safe, public places for safety.

4. No Pressure: Financial terms (free or paid) are decided between donor and the receiver only.

⚠️ Disclaimer:MediBridge is a neutral peer-to-peer exchange platform. We do not sell, verify, or take responsibility for any medicine listed. Users must consult a qualified doctor before consuming any shared medicines. Financial transactions, if any, are strictly between donor and receiver.
